# Pingo 1.0 TestFlight Notes

Thank you for testing Pingo, a 22-game collection built for Apple Messages.

Please focus on:

- finding and opening Pingo inside Messages
- sending and accepting a challenge between two physical devices
- returning turns through the newest Pingo card
- stale/older card behavior
- Single, Best of 3, and Best of 5 series
- 8-Ball, Chess, Sea Battle, Bowling, Draw & Guess, Crazy Eights, and Mini Racing
- profile, XP, streak, achievements, history, and friend records
- purchasing one game pack and confirming only that pack unlocks
- Restore Purchases after reinstall/sign-in
- Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Voice Control, dark/light appearance, rotation, and compact/expanded Messages presentation

When reporting a gameplay issue, include the game name, both devices/iOS versions, which player's turn it was, and whether the newest Pingo message card was opened.
