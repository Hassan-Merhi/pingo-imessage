# Pingo Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

Pingo is a multiplayer game collection for Apple Messages. This policy describes the information Pingo uses to provide profiles, matches, progression, and optional purchases.

## Information Pingo processes

Pingo may process a player identifier, user-selected username and avatar, authentication token hashes, gameplay and match state, game results, progression, achievements, streaks, and opponent history. Some game content is also carried in the Pingo iMessage cards exchanged by participants.

## Why Pingo uses this information

Pingo uses this information to provide multiplayer matches, maintain a player's profile and progression, prevent stale match updates, restore app state, and operate the service.

## Purchases

Optional game and cosmetic unlocks are purchased through Apple's StoreKit. Pingo uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine access to purchased content. Pingo does not operate its own payment-card system.

## Tracking and advertising

Pingo does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking in the current release and does not sell personal information for advertising.

## Service providers

Pingo uses infrastructure providers to operate its backend service. Production infrastructure may process technical request information as necessary to provide, secure, and diagnose the service.

## Data choices

Players can change their username/avatar in Pingo. Requests concerning account data or deletion should be sent through the support contact published with the App Store listing. Some data may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or service integrity.

## Children

Pingo is a family-friendly game collection, but the App Store age rating and regional requirements apply. Pingo does not intentionally add behavioral advertising or tracking for children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Pingo's features, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

## Contact

Use the support contact published on Pingo's App Store product page for privacy questions.
